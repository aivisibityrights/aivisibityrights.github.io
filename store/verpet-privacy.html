Privacy Policy — VerPet Carebook

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the VerPet Carebook mobile app (iOS and Android) published by AI Visibility Rights.

In short: VerPet Carebook has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no AI service. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you choose to share or export it.

1. Information the app stores

The app stores, only on your device, the information you enter yourself:

  • Pet passports: name, profile photo, species, breed, date of birth, microchip or ID, group/enclosure, allergies and cautions as entered by you, and additional notes. Passports can include third-party contact data you type, such as your veterinarian's or clinic's name and phone number and an emergency contact.
  • Care stories linked to each pet: the plan you typed and its revisions, questions, things to follow, start and appointment dates, archive periods and reminder settings
  • Care tasks: the pet, task name, instructions you enter, schedule, due dates and times, stopped status, and the history of done, skipped or not-recorded outcomes, notes and corrections
  • Local reminder information: notification identifiers, the last queued date and the timezone used to schedule task reminders. This timezone comes from the device's time settings; the app does not request location access.
  • Observations: date, time, the note you wrote, the value you chose (similar to usual, changed, not observed) and optional photos
  • Weights you record, with date, time and a note
  • Source files (PDF or image) you choose to attach from your device
  • App settings: language, appearance, and counters that decide whether the app may ask for a rating once

We do not receive or collect these entries on a server. The app has no user account; pet passports are local records you create. Contact details and other information you enter are included in the records you choose to export and may appear in a visit brief you share.

2. Photos and files

  • Pet profile and observation photos can be taken or chosen from your library. The camera is used and permission requested only when you tap Take photo. Microphone access is blocked and video is off. No photo leaves the app on this device unless you choose to export or share it.
  • Taken or chosen photos are resized, stripped of EXIF metadata and copied into the app's private storage on your device. Originals in your library are never modified or deleted.
  • Attached PDFs or images are copied into the app's private storage.
  • Deleting a story, deleting all data, or restoring a backup removes the corresponding files from the app's storage.

3. Notifications

If you save a reminder, the app asks for notification permission when needed and schedules local notifications on your device. Care-task reminders are queued ahead for a limited period and refreshed when the app is opened. Check the queued-through date and open the app regularly; reminders are not promised indefinitely in the background. Notification text can include the pet name and story or task title, subject to your device notification settings. No notification service of ours is involved.

4. Sharing and backups

  • Sharing a visit brief (text or PDF) or a backup opens your device's share sheet. You choose the recipient. The app itself sends nothing.
  • Backup records include pet passports and the contact details you entered, linked care stories, plans, questions, observations, weights, care tasks and their outcome history.
  • A complete backup includes photos and attached files as well as records, within the app's transfer limits. When the photos and files are larger than one backup file can hold, the backup is saved as several numbered part files. Each part can contain photos and files, so keep all parts together and private. If some files cannot be included, a partial backup contains the available files and lists the names of the omitted files and why they were omitted. The app asks you to confirm before exporting a partial backup.
  • A records-only file contains no photos or attached files. It can be offered, with confirmation, when a file bundle cannot be made. The separate records-only text export also excludes files.
  • Exports, including backups and visit briefs, are unencrypted. They may contain pet details, your notes and third-party contact information. Review them, keep them private and choose recipients carefully.
  • Restoring a backup replaces the Carebook records on this device. For a backup in parts, you add each remaining part after choosing the first. If you restore before adding every part, all records are restored, but the photos and files in the missing parts are not. Review the summary and any omitted-file list before confirming. Files included in the backup are restored; files listed as omitted cannot be recovered from that backup.
  • Backups do not transfer active notification schedules or the task reminder timezone. Reminders must be reviewed and re-enabled after a restore on the receiving device.
  • Device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups) follow your operating system settings and are outside the app's control.

5. Network activity

The only network activity is the standard Expo over-the-air update check, which lets us deliver fixes to the app's JavaScript without a store release. This request contains no user data. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the app.

6. Purchases

VerPet Carebook is a one-time paid download handled by the App Store or Google Play. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Payment information is processed by the store, never by the app.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It is rated for all ages and contains no accounts or social features.

8. Your rights

Because the app keeps no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can delete a story from its detail screen, a care task together with its outcome history from the task, an empty pet profile from its passport, or all Carebook data from My data. Deleting or relinking a story keeps the pet profile photo. Removing or replacing that photo deletes its unreferenced file only after the record is saved. Uninstalling removes the app's private storage; copies you exported or shared remain wherever you saved or sent them.

9. Not medical advice

VerPet Carebook records the information you enter. It does not diagnose, assess urgency, recommend treatment, interpret symptoms or replace veterinary advice. Follow your veterinarian's instructions and contact them with any medical question.
