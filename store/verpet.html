What's inside

Keep the details you want to tell your vet together, from daily observations to the next appointment.

A passport that connects the stories

  • Pet profile photo, identity, breed, birthday, microchip, vet contacts and cautions you enter yourself
  • Link several stories to the same pet, each with its own plan, questions and dates
  • Name groups, cages or tanks; search pet selectors by name, species, breed, group or microchip
  • Cats, dogs, birds, rabbits and other companions; enter your own species name when needed

Care tasks with an honest history

  • Set tasks for once, daily, weekly or a chosen day interval
  • Record done or skipped. Missing entries stay not recorded, and corrections remain in the history

Reminders you can review

  • Optional reminders are queued ahead for a limited period and refreshed when you open the app. Check the queued-through date and reopen regularly
  • The plan is your vet's, entered in your words. The app never suggests medication or doses

Weight over time

  • Log weights in grams, kilograms or pounds with the date, time and a note
  • See weight across the linked stories in a combined chart, including entries recorded in different units

A visit brief that is ready when you are

  • Choose the whole-pet brief or a single-story brief; both include the pet passport
  • The whole-pet brief brings active and archived stories together with care-task history and a combined weight chart
  • Each story keeps its own coverage. Calendars show gaps, and overlapping days are not added together
  • Share text or a PDF with the pet profile photo and observation photos through your device's share sheet. You pick the recipient

Your data stays with you

  • No account, cloud sync, ads, tracking or AI service
  • Passports, care tasks, notes, weights and files stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them
  • Complete backups include records, photos and attached files within transfer limits. Partial backups name omitted files; a records-only file contains no photos or attachments
  • Review the backup before restoring: it replaces the Carebook data on this device. Re-enable reminders afterwards
  • Exports are unencrypted. Keep them private and save a backup before changing phones or uninstalling
  • Take or choose photos. The camera is used only when you tap Take photo; no microphone or video. Photos stay in the app on this device unless you export or share them. Attach existing PDFs or images for reference
VerPet Carebook records the information you enter. It does not diagnose, assess urgency, recommend treatment, interpret symptoms or replace veterinary advice. Follow your veterinarian's instructions and contact them with any medical question.

Questions

Does the app tell me if my pet is sick?

No. It keeps your own observations organised and honest so you can tell your vet what actually happened. Contact a veterinarian for any medical concern.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no server of ours. Export a backup before changing phones or uninstalling.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pay once, use forever. No in-app purchases and no ads.

Which languages?

English and Thai, with more languages included in the app.

Can I keep several stories for the same pet?

Yes. Link stories deliberately to a pet passport, then share a whole-pet brief. Pets with matching names are never linked automatically. Groups or enclosure names and search help you find the right pet.

Will reminders keep running without opening the app?

Care-task reminders cover a finite queued period. Check the queued-through date and open the app regularly to refresh them. You must enable reminders again after restoring a backup.

What is in a backup?

A complete backup includes records, photos and attached files within transfer limits. A large one is saved as several part files; keep every part together. A partial backup lists files that could not be included. A records-only file has no photos or attachments. Exports are unencrypted; keep them private.
