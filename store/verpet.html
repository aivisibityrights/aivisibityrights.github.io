What's inside

Everything a careful owner needs between visits. Nothing that pretends to be a vet.

A care story for each pet

  • Cats, dogs, birds, rabbits and any other companion, each with a photo
  • Keep your vet's plan in your own words and the questions for next time
  • Choose the things to follow: meals, energy, a healing wound
  • One-time or daily reminders from your device

Weight over time

  • Log weights in kilograms or pounds with the date, time and a note
  • A gentle chart shows the trend, so the conversation with your vet is about a line, not a single number

A visit brief that is ready when you are

  • Days with entries, days observed, days not observed and days without an entry, shown separately
  • A four-week calendar that makes gaps visible instead of hiding them
  • Photos and weights included in the PDF
  • Text or PDF through your device's share sheet. You pick the recipient

Your data stays with you

  • No account, no cloud sync, no ads, no tracking and no AI service
  • Notes, photos and weights are stored only on your device; you choose what to share and with whom
  • Export a backup as text or a file, and restore it after checking what it contains
  • Photos come from your library only; the app never asks for the camera
VerPet Carebook records the information you enter. It does not diagnose, assess urgency, recommend treatment, interpret symptoms or replace veterinary advice. Follow your veterinarian's instructions and contact them with any medical question.

Questions

Does the app tell me if my pet is sick?

No. It keeps your own observations organised and honest so you can tell your vet what actually happened. Contact a veterinarian for any medical concern.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no server of ours. Export a backup before changing phones or uninstalling.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pay once, use forever. No in-app purchases and no ads.

Which languages?

English and Thai, with more languages included in the app.
