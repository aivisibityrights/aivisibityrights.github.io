WORKING ON A PROJECT

  • Create a project for each job and add its number and address. You can enter your name, license and organization in Settings.
  • Link supported members to carry loads from a slab to its supporting beam. Recompute Project follows the load path and reports members that need your input.
  • Review the status beside each member. NOT CHECKED flags an unresolved check. VALUES ONLY identifies informational results. If a saved result is marked STALE, reopen it, review its inputs and recalculate before using it.

CONCRETE AND FOUNDATIONS

  • Check one-way and two-way slabs in separate tools. Review beam flexure, shear and stirrup spacing, or inspect a column's axial and biaxial capacity using the Bresler reciprocal load method. Serviceability results depend on the slab method and the data supplied.
  • Check shear-wall flexural strength at the applied axial load using strain compatibility. Review precast planks at construction and composite stages when the required manufacturer data is available.
  • Review bearing pressure and punching shear for isolated footings. Add soil-layer data for an optional settlement estimate. The European method checks straight-bar anchorage against the length available. Separate tools cover combined footings, pile caps and single-pile capacity.

STEEL AND CONNECTIONS

  • Check steel beams and columns using supported rolled sections. A slender section outside the selected column method's limits is marked NOT CHECKED.
  • Enter plate and bolt dimensions to check a bolted connection, or specify a supported longitudinal fillet weld. Base plate results identify concrete anchorage as NOT CHECKED.

LOADS AND OTHER TOOLS

  • Estimate seismic base shear and floor forces using the equivalent lateral force method. Wind options include US and European procedures and Thai provincial wind data.
  • Check gravity and cantilever retaining walls for sliding, overturning and bearing under the specified soil and loading conditions.
  • Find section area, centroid and moments of inertia. Torsion and plastic properties are available for supported shapes.
  • Size pressure pipes or gravity drains. With pump and suction data, estimate the head required and the available suction head.
  • Estimate lighting from room use, fixture output and layout. The uniformity estimate uses fixture spacing and mounting height.

REVIEWING RESULTS

  • Inspect the beam diagram and compare the demand and capacity shown for individual checks. For designs with quantity estimates, review the concrete and reinforcement before using a total.
  • Start with the worked example or clear the inputs. Save eligible results to the project, or use Save to History and reopen them from the Inbox.
